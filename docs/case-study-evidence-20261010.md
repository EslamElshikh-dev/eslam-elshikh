## Interpretation

The public comparisons describe observed metrics, including the declines in Tawod engagement rates. They do not isolate the causal effect of design, SEO, advertisements or individual posts. Contact clicks and key events are defined as recorded interactions, rather than received calls, bookings, customers or contracts. Product administration images remain demonstration views.

The implementation adds no new client identifiers, private account exports, spending figures, patient information or tracking scripts. The original full reports remain outside the published website. The public pages show only the selected aggregate metrics, their periods, source names, interpretation and the public website captures.
